# Update: new characters, a fuller island with regions, wildflowers

This update draws on reference pictures for direction: a dense, varied island, lush meadows with flowers, and adult-proportioned heroes. Everything is original to Dropzone: no map layout, character, outfit or art is copied from another game.

## Characters
- **Adult proportions:** players are about seven and a half heads tall, instead of the old big-headed look.
  - Long legs, with the hips at 1 m.
  - A narrow waist widening to broad shoulders.
  - Longer, slimmer arms.
  - A smaller head, still about 1.85 m tall overall.
- **Faces:** adult features: almond eyes with a lash line, a stronger jaw and chin, and a proper nose and brows.
- **New outfit pieces:**
  - A long field coat with tails and lapels.
  - A panelled bodysuit with stripes down the sides, arms and legs.
  - A tank top.
  - A hood worn up (with a dark inside), a cloth face mask, reflective bands and a cape.
- **Roster:**

  | Outfit | Look |
  |---|---|
  | Trailblazer | explorer in a field coat |
  | Tidepool | track jacket and cap |
  | Moss | forest ranger: hooded coat and mask |
  | Ultraviolet | neon racer in a bodysuit |
  | Ember | rescue crew: reflective bands, goggles |
  | Glacier | parka |
  | Citrus | tank top and shades |
  | Midnight | shadow operative: bodysuit, hood and mask |

  The boss crew now wear capes.
- **Gameplay:** unchanged. The hitboxes are the same (the head still sits where the head hitbox is), and every animation carries over. Far-away players use the new proportions too.

## A fuller island
- **New places:** six more named places fill the empty stretches between towns, for 20 in all, plus the roadside hamlets. All are joined to the road network.
  - **Maple Hollow:** a camp in the autumn wood.
  - **Lookout Ridge:** a camp in the north.
  - **Petal Fields:** a farm of flower stripes.
  - **Blossom Grove:** a camp in the blossom forest.
  - **Redrock Gulch:** a quarry in the badlands.
  - **Frostfall Lodge:** a station on a new snowy peak.
- **Denser woods:** forests are much denser everywhere, so the land between places is woodland to fight through, not empty field.
- **Map canopy:** the map now draws every tree's crown in its own colour, so you can read the woods, the autumn wood and the blossom forest from the map.

## Regions
- **Autumn wood (north-east):** orange and gold trees on leaf-litter ground, climbing the big mountain's lower slopes, around the new **Amber Lake**.
- **Blossom forest (west):** pink and lilac blossom trees on deep green ground, around the old lake.
- **Red-rock badlands (south-west):** banded red ground, flat-topped mesas with steep sides, scrub and boulders, and no trees.
- **Second snowy peak (south-east):** about 134 m high and snow-capped, with Frostfall Lodge up on it.
- **Petal Fields:** long stripes of pink, yellow, violet, orange and white flowers.

## Ground and wildflowers
- **Drifts:** wildflowers grow in drifts across the meadows: patches of yellow, pink, purple or white, with a few strays elsewhere.
- **Stripes:** Petal Fields' flowers follow the stripes.
- **Badlands:** grass thins out to nothing on the red rock.

## Caves
- **Walkable entrances:** caves are only placed where you can walk in and out of both ends. There's no mesa wall or steep bank at a mouth, and no drop too deep for the steps up into it.
- **Where they are:** three caves, in the autumn wood, the badlands and the foothills of the new peak.

## Cost
- **Island:** about the same to generate, about 0.5 s.
- **Drawing:** the busiest views (the autumn wood) draw about 1.5 million triangles on Medium, up from about 1.3 million, because of the denser woods.
