# Update: Redeploy rules

Matches now use **Redeploy** rules instead of one life each: eliminated players drop back in from the sky.

## The rules
- **Solo:** you have 2 redeploys. The third elimination is out.
- **Duos, trios and squads:** when you're eliminated, you come back as long as at least one teammate is still standing.
  - Knocked teammates don't count as standing.
  - If everyone goes down, the team is out, and anyone waiting to redeploy is called off.
- **Knocks still work:** in teams you're knocked first, and teammates can revive you. Bleeding out or being finished counts as an elimination, and redeploy then applies.
- **When redeploys close:** once the storm reaches its last three circles (announced to everyone). After that, eliminated means out, the way it used to be.
- **Recall cards and beacons:** only after redeploys close. Before that you don't drop a card, since you come back on your own.

## Coming back
- **Countdown:** a 7-second countdown replaces the death card, showing how many redeploys you'll have left (solo) or that your team is still in.
- **Where you drop:** from 170 m up, over the current safe zone. In teams it's near a standing teammate (50–140 m away); in solo it's anywhere in the zone. Open your glider with Space.
- **Starter kit:**
  - A common or uncommon gun with two pickups' worth of ammo.
  - Three bandages.
  - The pickaxe.
  - A full overshield.
- **What you lose:** everything you carried drops where you went down, as before.
- **HUD:** a chip above your health bars shows **2 REDEPLOYS**, **1 REDEPLOY LEFT**, **REDEPLOY ON** (teams) or **REDEPLOYS CLOSED**. Toasts tell you when you or a teammate drop back in, and when redeploys close.

## Counting
- **Players left:** players waiting to redeploy still count as in the match, for players left, teams left and the win check. A solo player only gets their placement once they're out for good.
- **Kills:** every elimination credits the eliminator (and pays the 15 gold bounty), including eliminations of players who come back.

## Tests
- **Solo:** two redeploys from the sky with a gun, bandages and overshield, still counted in while waiting, then out on the third elimination with the right placement.
- **Duos:** you come back near your standing teammate, and the countdown is called off when the team goes down.
- **Closing:** redeploys close in the storm's last circles, and after that an elimination is final.
- **Full 80-bot matches:** still end with one winner once the storm closes. The kill bookkeeping counts eliminations in the feed, since a player can now be eliminated more than once.
